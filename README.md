# Creatobblemon : Create x Cobblemon

We always wanted to create a big factory making all the **Balls™** and **Fluids™** we wanted.
And for that, we made a combination of new items and crafts using Create's own crafting system.

---

# Additions

## Items

### Half Balls

To craft Pokéballs, you'll first need to press and saw a metal sheet to make Half Balls - the exception is diamond, because... well, you don't press diamond, so you'll need to cut and polish it.
![Half Balls Crafts](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/f82b82c4115eaf59d1328af366725c8dcf63b138.gif)

### Ball Button & Ball Lock

You will also need Ball Buttons for the modern Pokéballs, and Ball Locks for the ancient ones. The craft is quite simple, you either need to press a zinc nugget, or cut and press an andesite alloy.

![Buttons Crafts](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1abca2480740694d9677928e5ff022bae5208053.png)

### Blank & Ancient Blank Balls

The next step is crafting a Blank Ball ! To do that, you'll need to combine two Half Balls of the same material, press them together, and add a Ball Button or a Ball Lock depending on if you want to craft a modern Blank Ball, or an Ancient Blank Ball.

![Blank balls craft](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/6195b843bda2b1f03f4d094a89f666325de00c19.gif)

### Glass Vial & Pills *(X-Stat Items)*

With these crafts, X-Stats items are slightly more gainflu than when crafting them by hand. Though, as you'll need to bottle them individually, we added  the Glass Vial to contain them.

![All the pills crafts](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/208c6ea430057772957e18c64b1dfa8728e661d7.gif)

### Apricorn Shells

Apricorn Shells are used to craft most of the Pokéballs in this mod, you obtain four of them by sawing one Apricorn, and you need four of them to make one modern Pokéball, or two for an ancient one, corresponding to the colors used in the regular craft.

![Apricorn shells](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/a483b6d2239925b094667f42d34ca7b2650c12a9.png)

### Tumblestone Powders

In the same way, Tumblestone Powder is necessary to make Ancien Pokéballs, you'll obtain four of them by crushing them.

![tumblestone powder](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/04047487996947d60139bc68aaa4fd205aa18b75.png)

### Ball Blueprints

The last item to make Pokéballs is a Blueprint. You first make a blank one using a sheet of paper and a blue dye, and you can then create the one needed for a specific Pokéball by combining it with the said Pokéball - and if you ever need more of this Blueprint, you can combine a Ball Blueprint with a blank one to get two Ball Blueprints.

![blueprint](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/1ea9589684a8238d4722f321670822219d70905b.png)

![ball blueprints](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/8bb7d44f2cb5c90ea1a6bbf9eb48bd8012bfdd2b.png)

### Potion Mixtures

Using a heated mixer, you can make mixtures for all medicine and stat changing items that need to be contained in a Glass Bottle. Most of these crafts usually involve the same amount of items for an equivalent result.

![mixtures](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/07ff437d37576a913f307edc4d404a7d09761302.png)

## Recipes

This mods also allows you to craft a few items that are used in strategy - namely, the Exp. Candies, the Hyper Training Candies, the Mochis, and even the Ability Capsule and the Ability Patch ! To maker your life easier, we also made a lot of them a bit less expensive than they are if crafted by hand.

###  Sequenced assembly recipes for every vanilla Balls

![sequencing balls](https://cdn.modrinth.com/data/cached_images/baa8113f75048492740256b41e6c59949c4cf746.png)

## Other tweaks

One last thing this mod allows you to do is use the Mechanical Harvester to gather the Tumblestones from Cobblemon without having to replant them everytime - another little thing to make your life easier !

# ⚠️ Dependencies ⚠️

This mod has two dependencies:
- Cobblemon (> 1.8.X)
- Create (> 6.0.X)

# What's next ?

We have a few ideas in mind for future update, but they would be rather difficult to implement. First, allowing the Mechanical Harvester to collect Tumblestones is functional, but not really coherent, so we'll maybe make a machine dedicated to this, and possibly Type Gems and Amethysts... if we figure out how to code such a machine. Next, we were thinking of automating the craft of TMs, but in the same way, it would need a new machine, and with how many TMs there are, it would take quite a lot of time. Finally, for a smaller thing, we were hesitating on whether it'd be interesting enough or not to automatize all the food that Cobblemon adds.
One thing we don't plan to do though is automation for all the held and evolutionary items, we think that these aren't crafted often enough to have the need to craft them automatically.
If you have any additions you'd like to see, once again, please give us your thoughts !
